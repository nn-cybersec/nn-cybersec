About the event

The International Young Scientists School “Artificial Intelligence & Cybersecurity” will be held online on 20–23 October 2026. The program brings together key areas of Artificial Intelligence (AI) and Information Security (IS), including the security of autonomous AI systems, industrial applications of AI, AI-assisted search for scientific literature, computer vision, document analysis, AI model security, multi-agent system security, modern cryptanalysis methods, trustworthy artificial intelligence, cybersecurity of cyber-physical systems, neural network model optimization and protection, distributed software systems, continuous user authentication, AI-based protection of cultural heritage, and software reliability and security.

Organizer

Organizer: North-Caucasus Federal University (NCFU)

The event is financially supported by the Russian Science Foundation (project No. 25-71-30007) .
